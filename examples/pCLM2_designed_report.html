Aim

Add the lambda BoxB x8 array to the CMV-mCherry construct.

Cloning strategy

  • Backbone: pCLM3_pcDNA3.1_CMV-mCherry (5,961 bp), opened at 1,486–1,513.
  • Insert: Lambda BoxB x8, 300 bp.
  • Subclone with XhoI + XbaI — directional.
  • Product: 6,234 bp (300 bp in, 27 bp out).

Verified plan

Generated from the simulated and verified plan, reproduced here without alteration. The procedure below expands it; if the two ever disagree, this box is the one that was checked against the sequence.

1. Digest the vector with XhoI + XbaI and gel-purify the large backbone (5934 bp).
2. Digest the donor with XhoI + XbaI and gel-purify the 300 bp insert band. Extra cuts in the donor backbone are fine -- they only help separate the band.
3. Ligate (T4). The two vector ends are mutually incompatible, so the insert can only go in one way and the vector cannot self-close.
4. Screen across both junctions. The product is 6234 bp (300 bp in, 27 bp out).

5. Transformation, plating and colony picking

Thaw 25 µL of competent cells on ice and add 2 µL of the assembly. Carry the negative control through alongside it.

Recover in 500 µL SOC, 1 h shaking at 30 °C.

Plate 50 µL on selective agar and grow overnight at 30 °C — tandem repeats recombine out at 37 °C, so use a recA- strain and keep the culture short.

Pick 3 colonies, miniprep, and send for Plasmidsaurus whole-plasmid sequencing. Record the alignment and concentration below.

Screen across the upstream junction: the sequence there becomes AATAGCTCGAGG.

Screen across the downstream junction: the sequence there becomes GGCCCTCTAGAC.

Check the length of the Lambda BoxB x8 array on the sequencing read rather than assuming it survived — a recombined clone aligns cleanly, just shorter.
