Aim

A control for the NF-kB sensor: pcDNA3.1 carrying miniCMV-mCherry-LambdaBoxBx8-polyA with AmpR, i.e. the sensor cassette driven by the bare minimal promoter, so any mCherry signal is response-element-independent background.

Cloning strategy

  • Backbone: pHL391_pcDNA3.1_NFKBRE1-miniCMV-mCherry-LambdaBoxBx8 (5,770 bp).
  • Cut with MfeI + EcoRI, gel-purify the backbone, religate.
  • Removes NFKBRE, NFKB_Canonical1, NFKB_Canonical2, NFKB_Variant — 66 bp, of which 12 bp is collateral.
  • Product: 5,704 bp.

Assemble at room temperature in the order listed, flick to mix, spin down. Incubate at 37 °C for 5 min. Do not extend it: one of these enzymes shows star activity beyond 0.5 h, so a 'just to be sure' overnight digest cuts sites you did not plan for. MunI cannot be heat-inactivated — column-purify or phenol/chloroform-extract before ligating, rather than relying on a heat step. (80°C, 5min would handle the other(s).)

2. Gel-purify the backbone

Two fragments come off the digest: the 5,704 bp backbone you want and the 66 bp excised piece. Cut out the large band.

Excise the band and purify with the Zymoclean Gel DNA Recovery kit: weigh the gel slice and add 3 µL of ADB per mg. Record the gel mass and the eluate concentration below — later reactions are calculated from it.

3. Religate

This is an intramolecular ligation — the backbone closes on itself, so there is no insert and no molar ratio to set. Keep the DNA dilute (2–5 ng/µL in the reaction) so the ends find each other rather than another molecule.

Ligate with T4 DNA ligase at room temperature for 30 min. The junction becomes ACCGACAATTCT.

MfeI and EcoRI leave compatible ends, which is what allows the backbone to close. Do not dephosphorylate here — self-closure is the product.

Record at the bench

  • DNA in the ligation (ng)

4. Transformation, plating and colony picking

Thaw 25 µL of competent cells on ice and add 2 µL of the assembly. Carry the negative control through alongside it.

Recover in 500 µL SOC, 1 h shaking at 30 °C.

Plate 50 µL on selective agar and grow overnight at 30 °C — tandem repeats recombine out at 37 °C, so use a recA- strain and keep the culture short.

Pick 3 colonies, miniprep, and send for Plasmidsaurus whole-plasmid sequencing. Record the alignment and concentration below.

Screen across the new junction: the sequence there becomes ACCGACAATTCT.

Check the length of the Lambda BoxB x8 array on the sequencing read rather than assuming it survived — a recombined clone aligns cleanly, just shorter.
